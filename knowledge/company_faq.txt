Company FAQ

Q: What is the vacation policy?
A: Employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, accruing monthly.

Q: How do I request time off?
A: Submit requests through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

Q: What is the remote work policy?
A: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Q: When is the annual performance review?
A: Performance reviews happen every December.
